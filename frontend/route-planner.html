🧠 Smart Optimization Engine Architecture

🎯
Priority Nearest Neighbor

Urgent orders receive a 40% distance discount factor during candidate selection, ensuring time-critical packages are prioritized along the route.

🚦
Simulated Traffic Impact

Speed is adjusted by traffic factors (Low 1.0x, Med 1.2x, High 1.5x). Traffic delays dynamically extend travel time and increase route cost penalties.

⛽
Fuel & Mileage Modeling

Fuel consumption = Distance / Mileage. Estimates total fuel liters, total expense in ₹, and computes the exact fuel cost per delivery stop.

📊
Route Efficiency Score

Score = (Dist * 1.5) + Traffic Penalty + Fuel Cost - Urgent Bonus. A lower score indicates a more cost-effective sequence.
