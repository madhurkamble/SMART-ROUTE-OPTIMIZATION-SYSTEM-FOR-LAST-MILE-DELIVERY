📘 KPI Formulation & Logistics Methodology

🎯 On-Time Delivery Rate
(On-Time Completed / Total Completed) × 100

Measures customer SLA adherence and driver punctuality under planned ETA windows.

🚚 Vehicle Fleet Utilization
(Active In-Use Vehicles / Total Vehicles) × 100

Evaluates capital asset efficiency to prevent vehicles from sitting idle in logistics hubs.

⛽ Fuel Cost Per Delivery
Total Fuel Cost / Completed Deliveries

Tracks unit economics per package to maintain sustainable last-mile transport profit margins.

⚡ Route Efficiency Gain
((Baseline Distance - Optimized Distance) / Baseline) × 100

Quantifies kilometers and transit emissions conserved by Nearest Neighbor sequencing.
